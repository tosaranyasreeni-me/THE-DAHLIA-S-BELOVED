An Ode to Her Grace

Composed for Dahlia's Dearest
Shall I compare thee to the morning light,
Whose gentle beam doth break the sleeping skies?
Thou art more radiant to the waking sight,
And softer grace shines deep within thine eyes.

No artist’s brush could trace thy fair design,
Nor golden thread depict thy gentle form;
A quiet beauty, effortless, divine,
A tranquil harbor in the sudden storm.

Through every trial, thy perseverance stands,
Unbroken by the tempest or the tide;
A steadfast soul across the shifting sands,
Where elegance and silent strength reside.

Thy laughter breaks the darkest shadow’s shroud,
A melody that lingers soft and clear;
Exalted far above the restless crowd,
A light to cherish and a heart held dear.

Forever crowned in dignity and grace,
Time bows before the sovereign of this place.
